## Findings

The pooled success difference over 97 tasks is +1.0 points (95% CI -2.1 to +3.8), with a paired permutation p of 0.67, which is not significant at the 0.05 level. Per repo it ranges from -3.0 to +4.0 points.

Per repo, a run with the context files cost 1.21x to 1.74x as much as a run without (1.40x pooled, 95% CI 1.31x to 1.51x). Every repo's interval is above 1.0x, but 8 of the 582 runs have no cost data because they timed out and are left out of the cost figures. In pipecat the 6 runs without cost data could move the ratio anywhere from 0.78x to 1.74x, including below 1.0x; 3 are in the arm without context files and 3 in the arm with them. In pydantic-ai the 2 runs without cost data could move the ratio anywhere from 1.58x to 1.99x; all of them are in the arm with context files, so the ratio is likely an underestimate. Pooled, the ratio would lie between 1.18x and 1.63x if those runs cost anything from $0 to their budget.

With 22 to 25 tasks per repo, a single repo's runs would only reliably detect differences of about 5.4 to 13.6 points (80% power at the 0.05 level). These figures are estimated from the observed spread of per-task differences, so they are optimistic when almost all tasks tie. Per-repo bootstrap intervals on about 25 tasks tend to be too narrow; read them together with the paired permutation p.
